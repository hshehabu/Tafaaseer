Tafaseer App Icons

Four directions for the launcher icon, in the app's Dusty Green palette. Each is shown large, then at real launcher sizes on a light and a dark wallpaper, because an icon is judged at 48 px on a busy home screen, not at 200 px.

Concept A

Open mushaf المصحف

An open book with the round end-of-ayah mark above it. Reads instantly as a Quran app, even at small sizes. The most familiar of the four, and the least distinctive among other Quran apps.

Recommended Concept C

Ayah and sheet الآية والتفسير

The eight-pointed ayah rosette with a bottom sheet rising beneath it. It shows exactly what the app does: long-press an ayah, the tafsir slides up. Strong shapes on deep green hold up at 32 px and on both wallpapers.

Concept B

The letter taa ت

The first letter of تفاسير, its bowl holding two lines of text: the ayah held by its explanation. Light and quiet; the most minimal option. It needs the letter recognized, and it is the weakest on a light wallpaper.

Concept D

Wordmark tile تفاسير

The app's name in calligraphy inside a double mushaf frame, echoing the new surah headers. Elegant at large sizes and on the store page; the word gets hard to read below 48 px, so it suits a splash screen better than a launcher icon.

Drafts for choosing a direction. Once one is picked it gets a final drawing: an Android adaptive icon (separate foreground and background layers), a monochrome version for themed icons, and a store icon.
